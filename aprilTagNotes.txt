General outline for making an april tag processor:

Make objects of VisionPortal and AprilTagProcessor:
AprilTagProcessor.Builder() to set custom parameters and settings
VisionPortal.Builder() is fed the AprilTagProcessor we made and the physical webcam with hardwareMap
Use getDetections() with aprilTagProcessor for possible objects
Use for loop to iterate through detections
Check detections to see if it's what we're looking for and use its data
